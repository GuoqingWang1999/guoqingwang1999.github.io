Guoqing Wang is a fourth-year Ph.D. student at **[CS@Peking University](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/)**, advised by **Prof. [Dan Hao](https://sites.google.com/view/danhao/)** and **Prof. [Zeyu Sun](https://zysszy.github.io/)**. His research lies at the intersection of **Information Retrieval (IR)**, and **Software Engineering (SE)**, with a particular focus on the reliability, factual integrity, and development&evaluation of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** in complex environments.

## 🔬 Research Overview
His recent work explores how LLMs can be robustly integrated with external systems—ranging from **web search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** to automated software engineering workflows. A core objective of his research is building **rigorous evaluation methodologies** and cache-level defense frameworks to ensure models remain effective, trustworthy, and firmly aligned with verified knowledge bases. 

His current research interests focus on two synergistic directions:
1. **Web-Augmented LLMs & Information Retrieval**: Advancing the reliability of RAG pipelines, proactively mitigating search-induced distortions, and ensuring the knowledge alignment of LLMs when interacting with the open web.
2. **LLM-powered Software Engineering & Debugging**: Improving controllability in code generation and automated testing, while advancing rigorous fault localization (e.g., bug-inducing commit localization) to make debugging actionable for developers.
