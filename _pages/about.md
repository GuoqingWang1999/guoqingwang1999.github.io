## 🔬 Research Pillars
My research agenda is driven by the goal of making LLMs both **effective** for specialized domains and **trustworthy** in open environments.

### 1. AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)
Focuses on leveraging foundation models to automate and optimize the software lifecycle:
* **Automated Testing & Debugging**: Designing techniques for unit test generation and fault localization (e.g., bug-inducing commit localization).
* **Code Intelligence**: Improving the reliability and controllability of code generation and editing through prompt engineering and alignment training.

### 2. Truthful & Trustworthy AI
Focuses on the reliability and factual integrity of LLMs, especially in RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) scenarios:
* **Hallucination Mitigation**: Proactively detecting and fixing "search-induced distortions" where external noise overrides internal verified knowledge.
* **Knowledge Alignment**: Ensuring LLM outputs remain consistent with trusted knowledge bases (TKB) in high-stakes domains (Legal, Medical, Enterprise).
