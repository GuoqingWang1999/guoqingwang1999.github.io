Guoqing Wang is a fourth-year Ph.D. student at **[CS@Peking University](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/)**, advised by **Prof. [Dan Hao](https://sites.google.com/view/danhao/)** and **Prof. [Zeyu Sun](https://zysszy.github.io/)**. He got his bachelor’s degree from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2022.  His research lies at the intersection of **AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE)** and **Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI)**, with a particular focus on leveraging **large language models (LLMs)** to improve software development and maintenance workflows.

His recent work explores how LLMs can be used to support and automate software engineering tasks such as **code generation**, **unit test generation**, and **fault localization**, as well as how to build **rigorous evaluation methodologies** for these systems. More broadly, he is interested in bridging empirical software engineering with modern foundation models, aiming to make AI-assisted development both **effective** and **trustworthy**.

His current research interests focus on two directions:
1. **LLM-powered software engineering**: improving reliability and controllability in code generation, code editing, and automated testing, including prompt engineering, training objectives, and evaluation.
2. **Rigorous debugging and localization**: advancing fault localization and diagnosis (e.g., bug-inducing commit localization) to make debugging results more actionable for developers.
